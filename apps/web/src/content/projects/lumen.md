- Library grid that opens on your newest photos, with pinch-to-zoom columns and drag-select.
- The viewer zooms out of the tapped photo and flies back into its slot when you swipe down.
- Hidden album behind fingerprint or PIN, with screenshots blocked. No network access at all.
